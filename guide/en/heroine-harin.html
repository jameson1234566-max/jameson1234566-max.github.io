Rachel Adams guide

Rachel, the product planner on the next team. She loves change logs and spreadsheets. From meeting her to all 5 endings

Last updated 2026-11-20

Rachel Adams 📊Difficulty★★★★★

Product planner on the next team. The kind who keeps everything organized: requirements doc v14, a date log sheet, even a marriage roadmap.

Can marryLiving togetherNewlywedsParenting
In short
  • First message in week 3. Like Emily she is from the first company, so changing jobs cuts her off (she stays if she is at least a crush).
  • Her affinity before a crush rises a little faster than Emily's. Keep scheduling dates and you can go all the way to marriage.
  • Her dream home is the Dawn Bay new 3-bed flat (base $1.4M), so the newlywed ending costs a lot 💰

Rachel is the planner who confesses with 14 pros, 2 cons 😆 She estimates effort and rates your dates on a sheet, but she responds clearly to sincere replies. It plays out like Emily's route, so reading the shared rules first makes it easier.

How to meet her#

ItemCondition
CompanyThe first company (big company). Leave it before a crush and contact stops

Raising affinity#

One message comes every week from week 3.

  • Good reply: the first one, praising and looking after the meticulous Rachel. Affinity goes up, but it costs a little stamina and stress.
  • Bad reply: the last one, a cold no. In the first 5 messages it is -10, a big loss.
  • Legacy code is +1 but comes with reputation -10.
  • Her birthday 🎂 comes once a run; a gift is +8 (-$385).
  • From dating on, messages that cost money (like omakase) come. Paying yourself is the right reply, and on the day you opposed her proposal in a meeting, explain calmly and apologize.

Crush → confession → dating#

  1. Crush invite (affinity 80)I found a natural wine bar… → Sure. Wine talk, not spreadsheet talk.
  2. The crush stageDuring the crush stage affinity rises slowly. Keep up the dates.
  3. Confession (100)14 pros, 2 cons. Conclusion... I like you. → I'll fix the 2 cons. Yes. (declining = she leaves + new relationships locked)

Marriage conditions#

Date progress 60 + affinity 100 brings the proposal: Q1: meet the parents. Q2: wedding. After that: you, indefinitely. → Approved. Locking the schedule. (+10). Decline or leave it on read and it is -15 and never comes again. Married at week 100 means the marriage ending.

The newlywed offer comes within 15 weeks of marriage, 2 weeks after you buy the home. As the proposal gets close, build up a lump sum in the Invest tab ahead of time. Strategies are in the Investing page.

Fail and breakup conditions#

Exactly the same as Emily: never turn down the confession or proposal, left on read -15, a week with 0 dates -30, a week with 0 chores after marriage -30, weekend work -2. See the table in the Emily guide. If she is not yet a crush when you leave the first company, Rachel is gone.

FAQ#

Who is easier, Rachel or Emily?
Rachel's affinity before a crush rises faster than Emily's, but she starts lower at 5 and her first message is a week later. In the end they are about the same. If you aim for newlyweds too, Emily, with her cheap dream home ($208K), is far easier.
I want to protect my reputation in the opposed in a meeting message.
"The spec was weird" is reputation +10 but affinity -12. Not recommended in a dating run.
