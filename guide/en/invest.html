Investing guide

How the 10 stocks, 42 properties and 9 collectibles work and what they return, recommended strategies, patterns that go broke, and the investment endings

Last updated 2026-11-20

In short
  • 💰 Once your balance reaches $15.4K, the Invest tab's Stocks, Real estate and Collectibles all open at once.
  • 📈 The stock market is built so that buying with no information breaks even. The money goes to people who read the news source. Trust filings (90%); tabloids (50%) are a coin flip.
  • 🏠 Real estate is slow but steady (hold 80 weeks for roughly +25 to 50% on price alone, rent on top). One big boost can mean up to ×2.2 in 3 weeks.
  • ⌚ Collectibles are a roller coaster that can swing up to 25% in a week. Once bought you cannot sell for 10 weeks, and a new car loses 20% the moment you buy it.
  • 🏆 Investment endings: Super ant ($769K in stocks), 3 island owner endings (3 island listings), 6 heroine taste endings (everything she wants during newlywed life).

When does the Invest tab open? 🔓#

Invest in the tab bar starts out Locked. The moment your balance goes over $15.4K during a run (at a week close, or right away if you enter the Invest tab with that money), Stocks, Real estate and Collectibles open all at once, and if it opens at a week close the log shows 📈 Balance passed $15.4K! The Stocks tab is open. After that, all three markets move one week at every week close.

Sub-tabWhat you buyCharacterHow to sell
📈 Stocks10 stocksA game of skill where reading the news paysAny time, right away (0.15% fee)
🏠 Real estate42 fixed listings on the continent mapRises slowly and pays rentList it at an asking price (quick sale right away, -10%)
⌚ Collectibles9 items (up to 5 of each)Trendy goods that swing hard in a weekAt market price after 10 weeks of appraisal (before that, the pawnshop at 40%)
Good to know: Family support III

A run starts with a $2,308 balance. Raise the Family support run perk to level III (+$13.8K in total) and you start with $16.2K, so it opens the moment you enter the Invest tab in week 1. The earlier you start, the more overwhelming your edge for Super ant (table below).

Net worth and class 💳#

What the game calls net worth (total assets) is cash, stocks, real estate and collectibles all added up. It decides the class on your name card and your face on Home (the face props change in this order: bandage and sweat → nothing → glasses and tie → sunglasses and gold chain → monocle and mustache → a crown), and it opens the Cookie Isles ($3.8M).

How prices move#

A price moves as the sum of three things: base value, overheating and event hype.

  • Base value: goes up and down a little every week (noise), and is pulled slightly by each company's hidden trend this quarter. When the market mood is good, it rises along with it by its beta.
  • Overheating: the part where people overreacted to the news. 40% of it disappears every week. That is why a price that jumped a lot partly comes back.
  • Event hype: as a scheduled event approaches, the price rises in advance; the moment the result comes out, the hype vanishes and the result is priced in.

4 news sources: that's all there is 📰#

Every market news item carries a source label, and each source has a fixed chance of being right. Open Reading the market on screen and it says the same.

SourceChance it's rightWhen it appearsHow people react
Filing90%Event notices, early signals (40%), results, earningsThe result itself
Report78%Weeks 5 to 11 of a quarter, 16% a week per stockNormal
Tabloid50%5% per stock in weeks with no report, and event early signals (60%, right 55% of the time then)Biggest
Macro100%12% a week: AI investment boom, recession fears, a rate hike, hopes of a rate cutThe whole market mood

The market mood shows in 5 steps (Fearful, Weak, Neutral, Firm, Euphoric) and drifts back toward Neutral over time. The AI investment boom and recession move the mood a lot; rate news moves it a little. High-beta stocks like semiconductors, EVs and cloud get swept along the most.

The tabloid trap

A tabloid is right exactly half the time, yet people react to it more than to reports. Prices bought on a tabloid tend to come back as the overheating fades from the next week on. Buying stocks with a good tabloid tip usually ends at -7.7% after 80 weeks.

Earnings: they move on the gap from expectations#

Earnings come out at the week close every 13 weeks of your career (weeks 13, 26, 39, 52, 65, 78, 91). A stock's details show Earnings in week N, market expectations very low to very high.

  1. The hidden trendEach company has a hidden trend this quarter. Reports tell you its direction 78% of the time.
  2. Market expectationsExpectations move a little each time a report or tabloid comes out (less for tabloids). This is the value you see on screen.
  3. The releaseThe price moves by the gap between the real trend and market expectations, and more volatile stocks move more. A little overheating is added on top (it soon fades). If the real result is clearly better than expected it is an earnings surprise!, clearly worse an earnings shock…, and close to it in line with expectations.
  4. The next quarterIf this quarter was good, the market expects a lot from the next one too, but the real trend does not carry over that much. So after a blowout quarter, expectations run too high and a shock comes easily.
Tip

Late in a quarter (weeks 8 to 12), if reports have come out several times in the same direction but market expectations are still Normal, that is your chance. On the other hand, a stock that jumped right after earnings is already priced with overheating, so you are too late.

Scheduled events: the filing's early signal is the key ⭐#

When they have no event, Vaxwell Biotech, PixelForge Games, Bayport Shipyards, Glitzwood Pictures and Skyrack Cloud have a 6% chance every week of a results in 4 weeks notice. The result is already decided 50:50 the moment the notice comes.

Weeks leftWhat happensPrice
4 weeks (notice)Announced by filing, market-expected success rate 50%30% of the target hype priced in
3 weeks—50% priced in
2 weeksEarly signal: 40% are filings (right 90%), 60% are tabloids (right 55%). If positive, the market expects 65%; if negative, 35%75% priced in
1 week—100% priced in
ResultA success/failure filing; the hype vanishes and the result is priced into the base value (with a little overheating on top)Trial ±29.7%, order ±16.2%, new game ±15.0%, comeback ±13.9%

Hype grows the higher the success rate the market expects, with a small premium on top. For the trial, at an expected 50% the price right before the result is about +3.9%, at 65% +12.4% and at 35% -5.4%. The market moves the same 65%/35% whatever the signal's source, so only those who check the source profit.

The standard play
  • When a notice appears, buy and ride the rising hype (it climbs as the release gets closer).
  • If the signal 2 weeks before is a filing + positive, hold through the result (90% success).
  • If the signal is a tabloid or negative, sell before the result. A positive tabloid is only right 55% of the time and comes with a premium baked in, so holding loses on average.

Fees, day-trade tax, dividends#

  • Fees: 0.15% each when you buy and when you sell.
  • Day-trade tax: 0.2% more if you sell within 2 weeks of your last buy. Buying more restarts the 2 weeks (the stock's details show ⚠ You just bought this).
  • Dividends: Liberty Eagle Bank, Prairie Crunch Foods, Bellwave Telecom and Silicore Semiconductor pay dividends every 4 weeks after your first buy on everything you hold at that moment. The holding count does not reset when you buy more; sell everything and it starts over. Dividends received count as realized P&L.
Good to know: topping up right before a dividend

The dividend count starts from the day you first bought and pays on everything you hold at that moment, so buy just 1 share in advance and buy big the week before the dividend, and you still get the full 4 weeks' dividend. Check 💰 Dividend N% every 4 weeks held (N weeks to the next) in the details.

Quick trade buttons are Buy max, Buy 50%, Sell 50%, Sell all for each stock, and the account card has Sell every holding and Sell half of every holding. The gear that removes fees is Can't stop day trading~.

Retail sell-off: $154K in one stock is the limit 💣#

If one stock is worth $154K or more, the order book is thin. Every week there is a set chance that retail investors dump it and the price falls, and luck (including Seven parts luck) cannot stop it.

Value in one stockWeekly chanceFall when it hits

The more you put into one stock, the higher both the chance and the fall. If a quick buy would push you over $154K, a confirm box comes first.

Luck: rolled every time you open the Stocks tab 🍀#

If you hold stocks and it has been 1 week or more since your last look, opening the Stocks tab brings up the Your stocks since you last checked report. Luck is then applied to each stock's move against the price you last saw (your average buy price the first time). Luck is rolled only once a week, so opening it again the same week does not roll again.

Luck 10 (default)Seven parts luck (luck 70)
Stocks that fellOnly when down -5% or more, a 5% chance of being rescued to +4%Any drop at all: 100% rescued to +6%
Stocks that rose (+2% or more)4% chance the rise is ×1.528% chance the rise is ×4.5
CapUp to +60%. Luck fades from $769K in stocks (half at $1.5M) and is gone at $2.3M or more: Your account is too big for luck to help
ExceptionNo luck for a stock hit by a Retail sell-off in the meantime

When luck applies, the price really becomes that value (the base value changes too, so it does not slip back on its own). Spread your buys over 10 stocks and check every week with luck 10, and after 80 weeks you are usually up +13.0%; never open it and it is +3.9%. Even at luck 10, opening it every week pays.

How prices move#

A listing's price is set by the area market, the type market, a small wobble for each listing, and boosts.

  • Area market: over the long run it follows a steadily rising trend. About +38% over 80 weeks, so just holding makes money.
  • Type market: each type moves up and down a little and tends to come back to where it was.
  • Listing wobble: moves a little every week but never strays far.
  • A listing moves at most 8% in a week (up to 35% while a big boost is coming in).

On the map, next to an area's name you see 🔥 Overheated (a boost under way), ▲ Rising (area market over 4% above its usual trend), ▼ Falling (over 4% below) or Flat. The area market returns to its usual trend, so a falling area tends to come back up.

Real estate news and how much to trust it#

2 to 4 news items come every week. Labels are Official (always true), Likely (70%) and Rumor (35%), drawn 1 : 2 : 2. Fake Likely and Rumor items get a denied follow-up the next week and do nothing.

For Likely and Rumor you can wait and see whether a denial comes the next week (if it is true, you see the second rise). 4 rules to know#

  1. 10 weeks of appraisalFor 10 weeks after buying it is 🔒 under appraisal and you cannot sell at full price (5 weeks with Appraiser Friend). Sell in a hurry and you must hand it to the pawnshop for far below market (less of a loss with the Pawnshop VIP Card).
  2. Up to 5 of one itemYou can only buy up to 5 of the same item (🚫 No hoarding!). With VVIP High Roller, up to 15.
  3. A new car loses money the moment you buy itNew cars like the sports car and camper van cost more than market to buy, and lose a little value every week after. They are things to buy when you want one, not to grow your money.
  4. Prices swing but come backThey move a lot within a week, but over time each item gets pulled back to its set trend. Sell in a week it shot up, buy in a week it plunged: that is the basic rule.

Reading today's tabloid#

Every week 3 to 5 items get headlines. Sources are Tabloid (45% true), Exclusive (65%) and Breaking (85%), drawn 2 : 2 : 1, and 55% are rising news.

  • The moment a headline appears, that item moves 6% up or down whether it is true or not (you see the price already moved).
  • The next week, only news that was true moves once more: a rise goes up another 8 to 22%, a fall drops another 8 to 20%.

TOP 7 patterns that go broke 💀#

1. Chasing the hot stock

Putting everything into last week's top stock. You are paying top price for overheating (40% of it vanishes every week), so you are usually down -42.9% after 80 weeks.

2. Trusting a tabloid and holding through the event result

A positive tabloid is only right 55% of the time, yet the price already carries the success hype and a premium. You lose on average.

3. All in on one stock

From $154K, Retail sell-offs come (up to a 50% weekly chance of -20%), and luck cannot stop them. Holding only Vaxwell Biotech gives an 80-week median of -4.1%, with the bottom 10% at -68.8% (48.0% end up in profit).

4. Buying new cars as an investment

A sports car loses 20% the moment you buy it, then trends -1.2% every week. Held 100 weeks with no gear, the median is -75.0%. Only buy one for Brooke's taste or when you have Trendsetter.

5. Buying collectibles and suddenly needing cash

During the 10 weeks of appraisal the pawnshop pays 40%. When living costs are tight, keep out of collectibles.

6. Never opening the Invest tab

Stock luck and Trendsetter's effect on collectibles apply when you open the tab. Buy Seven parts luck or Trendsetter and leave the tab closed, and the effect drops sharply.

7. Trying to sell every home while living together and getting stuck

While living together or married you cannot sell 1 home, our home. If you want to clear out homes, sell the others first.

FAQ#

The Invest tab won't open.
You need a cash balance of $15.4K or more. It is the balance, not your stock holdings. It opens at a week close, or when you enter the Invest tab holding that money.
Can't I see a stock's hidden trend?
No. But reports (78% accurate) tell you the direction, so compare them with market expectations on screen.
When is an event's result decided?
The moment the notice appears. The early signal 2 weeks before tells you that result correctly 90% of the time if it is a filing, 55% if it is a tabloid.
Does Seven parts luck work on real estate and collectibles too?
It does nothing for real estate. For collectibles, together with Trendsetter it raises the Resell frenzy chance from 30% to 70% and gives the ⭐ Sure thing mark.
Where are the real estate fire sales?
Listings are now a fixed list, so no fire sales come up. To buy cheap, go for ▼ Falling areas (they tend to come back up to the usual trend).
Do I have to hold the island to the end of the run for its ending?
Owning the 3 even once at a week close or after a reply records it as Ending met! But it only counts once you finish the run to week 100.
When do dividends come in?
Every 4 weeks from the week you first bought, as cash at week close (log: 💵 Dividend). Sell everything and buy again, and the count starts over.
