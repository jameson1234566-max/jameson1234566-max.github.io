Gear and supplies guide

Prices, effects and the best time to buy for all 37 gear pieces and 18 supplies, plus a tier list of what to buy and what to skip

Last updated 2026-11-22

In short
  • 🎒 Gear is bought once with coins and kept for good (every run after that). Start with the basic set that costs 6,000 to 40,000 coins a piece (114,000 coins in total).
  • 🥤 Supplies are bought with run money ($) and get pricier as the weeks go by (at week 100 they cost 8 times as much as at week 1). Whatever is left disappears when the run ends.
  • 💘 Fated encounter (1,500,000 coins) is the key gear for Brooke: it lets you meet her in week 1 (how it ties into marriage is a spoiler on the Brooke guide). 🍀 Seven parts luck is the shortcut to Super ant.
  • 📖 Gear gives no codex rewards. You lose nothing by buying only what you need.

First, how the shop works 🛒#

Tap the Shop button on Home (or the coin count at the top) to open the shop. The tabs go Offers, Season pass, Card packs, Gear, Supplies, and today we look at the last two. The Gear and Supplies tabs only open while a run is in progress (with no run you see Start a career to use this.). Each page shows 8 items, and you turn pages at the bottom.

Using items from the Bag#

Tap the Bag button at the top of Home to see folds for Supplies (how many, plus a Use button) and Equipped gear. Buying supplies does nothing by itself: they work only once you tap Use here.

Reselling gear (10%)#

Tap Sell on a gear card and a blue confirm box opens: you get 10% of the price back in coins. The effect is gone right away in this run, and in the next runs too. If you already used a once-per-run effect (Calm down, buddy and the like), buying it again does not bring it back: it stays used for that run.

Careful

The Rubber duck, Desk plant and Mechanical keyboard from the Welcome kit run perk (CP 200/400/600) are gifts for that run only, so they have no Sell button. If you already bought them with coins, you do not need the Welcome kit.

Tier list: what should I buy first? 🏆#

Coins come in at about 2,000 to 2,900 per finished run, 1,000 per check-in and 2,000 per daily quest (How to get coins). That is why the order matters. All 37 pieces together cost 13,196,000 coins.

Heroine gear 💌#

Gear you only need when you aim for dating, marriage or juggling endings. Buy it when you have that goal, whatever its tier. The effects are in Love gear below.

Good to know

Gear bought during a run works from that same week. No need to wait for the next run. And since gear gives no codex rewards, there is no reason to buy everything except the joy of collecting (only the Gearhead achievement needs all 37).

Try it first in a trial run 🧪#

Before buying expensive gear, you can borrow it for 1 run. Once you play a 100-week run to the end, 🧪 Trial run opens at the bottom of the run start screen on Home.

SetGear insideTries

  1. Pick a setChoose the set to try on the run start screen. The tries you have left are shown with it.
  2. Confirm the startWhen you tap start, the rules are shown once more. The moment you tap Start trial, 1 try is used.
  3. PlayA trial run banner stays at the top of Home for the whole run, and borrowed gear carries a 🧪 Trial mark next to its name.
What a trial run cannot do
  • No ending rewards, run coins or CP, and nothing is recorded in the codex. No achievements unlock either.
  • Borrowed gear cannot be sold and disappears when the run ends.
  • Even if you End this run early, the try you used does not come back.
Tip

If you like a piece during the trial, you can buy it right away in the shop. Bought gear loses the Trial mark and stays yours. We suggest using the Investor set to feel how strong the gear combo is first, then buying what you need most.

Stamina, stress and focus gear 💪#

All of these apply every week at the end of the week. Each company adds base stress every week: big company +2, startup +4, foreign company +3, freelancer +1, Morning Commit +2. The plant, headphones and massage chair alone already cut more than the big company's base.

Trap: Gym membership ≠ Exercise

The Gym membership does not count as an Exercise slot in your schedule. It has nothing to do with the workouts needed to meet Brooke (500) or Fated encounter's week with exercise condition. The $38 fee goes out every week, which hurts more than you think early as an intern (weekly pay around $385).

Useless in a run where you divorced

After a divorce, stress does not go down until the end of that run (it never drops below 70), and focus does not go up either. So stress gear such as the plant, headphones, massage chair and Imaginary Quit Button, plus the Blue-light glasses and World-Muting Earplugs, do almost nothing. Stamina gear still works.

Tip: the Imaginary Quit Button is the best value there is

At -10 every week, stress goes down each week even at a startup (+4). If you pile up commits with all-nighters and overtime, it is worth every one of its 180,000 coins.

The Laser scalp comb only stops hair lost to stress every week. It cannot stop the 5 lost to a burnout. On the other hand, when you aim for the Shiny forehead achievement (hair under 10), you may need to sell the comb for a while.

Spoiler: the hidden hair ending and the scalp comb

If you aim for the A full head of hair ending (hair 100 for all 100 weeks), the comb plus never burning out is the combo. Dropping below 100 even once is a fail, and the Hair tonic cannot undo it.

Supplies 🥤#

Supplies get pricier as the weeks go by#

Supply prices go up with the week. In the same week everyone pays the same, whatever the company or salary. They rise slowly at first and faster later on, and at week 100 they cost about 8 times week 1. Buy the Regular customer run perk and they get 5%, 10% or 15% cheaper.

Tip: buy while cheap and stock up in your Bag

Supplies you buy go into the Bag and stay until that run ends, and they work the same whenever you use them. So buying a few Energy drinks, Protein shakes or Spa nights early, while prices are low, pays off (they vanish when the run ends, so not too many).

FAQ#

Does gear work in the run I bought it in, partway through?
Yes. Even if you buy it partway through a run, it works from the moment you buy it. And gear stays yours once bought, so it applies from the start of the next runs with no need to buy it again. There is no equipping step either.
Can I carry supplies over to the next run?
No. When the run ends, the supplies in your Bag disappear. Buy only as many as you need.
Do supply prices in the shop depend on my salary?
No. They depend only on the week (and Regular customer). In the same week, a big company employee and a freelancer pay the same.
Do the perfume and pickup notebook boost affinity from dates too?
In the current game they only boost affinity gained from message replies. Affinity from dates is unchanged.
What if I sell gear and buy it again?
You pay the full price again, and the sale only gave you 10%. In a run where you already used a once-per-run effect, buying it again does not bring it back for that run.
Is there a gear section in the codex?
Gear gives no codex rewards. Collecting it all only earns the Gearhead achievement.
Are these the same as the headphones, monitor and keyboard from the All-Nighter Tower?
No. Those are all-nighter-only gear you get by clearing All-Nighter Tower floors (All-Nighter Tower). They are separate from shop gear.
