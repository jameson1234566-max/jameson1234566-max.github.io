Claire guide

A secret office romance with Claire, your team lead at the startup. Three coworkers' suspicion, secret dates under fake titles, how not to get caught, and the odds of The Perfect Crime

Last updated 2026-11-20

Claire 🤫Difficulty★★★★★

Your team lead at the startup. At work you have never met before, and her message titles always read [Work] …. A secret lover who only talks casually in the stairwell.

Can marryNo living togetherNo newlywedsNo parenting
In short
  • Join the startup, PivotLabs and she contacts you in your first week (startup offer: week 12 on, Coding 90).
  • 3 coworkers (Jordan the CTO, Sam the lead, Riley the newest dev) each have their own suspicion, and if even one hits 100, you are caught and it is over.
  • Things move fast: join early and schedule 3 or more date slots, and you can easily make it to marriage.
  • The key is just one thing: always smooth it over naturally in suspicion messages. Do that and your chance of getting caught is 0%, with The Perfect Crime at 99.6%. Slip up even 1 time in 10 and the chance of getting caught is 41.8% 😱

Dating Claire is a messenger spy thriller 🕵️ Every 2 weeks Claire asks you on a secret date under a fake title like [Work] Meeting Notes Review Request, and every 3 weeks the coworkers probe with What's going on between you two? Smoothing things over costs a fair bit of stamina and stress, though, so managing your condition is this route's real difficulty.

Spoiler: how you first met Claire

Claire is someone you met on a blind date the weekend before you joined. Her first message opens with …Is that really you? Last Saturday. The blind date. So from the start you agree that at work you have never met before.

Trap: leaving a suspicion message on read is +30

Suspicion messages come like everyday messages, so they are easy to miss. One left on read is +30, so a coworker who started at 60 catches you after just 2. Always check your messenger every 3 weeks.

Secret dates (every 2 weeks)#

ReplyResult
Accept (70%)Affinity +8 to +15
Accept (30%, goes wrong)1 coworker sees you: suspicion +7 to +12, affinity +2 to +5

What if you keep declining secret dates? You never reach the crush stage. The -15s pile up and keep affinity from reaching 80. The suspicion from a failed date is mostly wiped out by one smooth-over (-3 to -8), so taking the dates pays off. Dates are her main source of affinity.

Raising affinity#

  • Good reply: for her first message, You never answered when I asked you out again… is the best. Her weekly everyday lines give +1 to +2 with the first reply, but cost stamina and stress.
  • Bad reply: cold replies are -3 to -5, leaving her on read is -20.
  • The biggest gains come from the secret dates every 2 weeks (+8 to +15). Date slots in your schedule work too, but they fill slowly during the crush stage.

Crush → confession → dating#

  1. Crush invite (affinity 80, when you are not dating anyone)[Work] 1:1 Meeting Schedule → Heading to the stairwell now. I'll answer in person (+5). I don't think it can work with you, boss… makes her leave.
  2. Confession (100)[Work] Final Approval Request Title: "Let's date." → Approved. With no end date or I like you too. But I wanted to be the one to say it first (both +5, stress -5). …I'll have to reject it means she leaves, plus the lock.

Marriage conditions#

Date progress 60 + affinity 100 → [Work] Long-Term Contract Renewal Term: for life → Renewed. For life (+10, stress -8). I'm not ready yet is -15, and it never comes again. Even after marriage the secret goes on, so you keep managing suspicion until week 100.

Living together, newlyweds, parenting#

None of the three. You are a secret couple, so no move-in offer, newlywed life or baby offer comes. Also, if you live with another heroine, Claire does not contact you.

Spoiler: how it ends if you get caught

If you are caught, Claire ends it saying If I want to stay a team lead, this is the right call, and if you were married she hands in her resignation that week. When you leave the startup, the ring comes back by courier with a note reading [Work] Final approval complete… 😢

FAQ#

Where do I see suspicion?
At the top of Claire's chat, a 🔍 Coworkers' suspicion (100 = caught) gauge shows all 3. The replies to suspicion messages also show the change in advance, like Jordan suspicion -5.
Smoothing things over eats too much stamina
Every 3 weeks the 3 messages come all at once, so stamina -30 to -90 can pile up in one week. Put rest in that week's schedule or use supplies. The moment you pick a clumsy reply to save stamina, suspicion jumps +15 to +25.
Does Claire count for Casanova and Octopus?
No. She counts in the juggling odds, but not in the head count for Casanova, Octopus or the group chat exposé.
