Zoe Martin guide

Zoe, junior QA and subscriber #1, only for the Dev influencer title. From your first video to marriage, living together, newlyweds and parenting, plus every title story trap

Last updated 2026-11-20

Zoe Martin (@bugcatcher_zoe) 🐞Difficulty★★★★★

Subscriber #1 on your channel and a junior QA. She finds bugs in vending machines, menus, even her mom's phone, and writes repro steps for everyday life.

Can marryLiving togetherNewlywedsParenting
In short
  • She appears only in runs with the Dev influencer title equipped. The title opens with the Video creator ending (side 100 + reputation 80).
  • You can only meet her if you upload the video in the week 2 title message. Pick Maybe later and all 3 subscribers are gone ⚠️
  • Keep scheduling dates and you can go all the way to marriage. Her dream home is the Dawn Bay station studio (base $177K), the cheapest of the subscribers 💰

Zoe is the one who leaves a First! comment on your first video 😆 She confesses with Bug report #100 and proposes with Bug report #365: QA to the very end. Her rules before the crush, during the crush and while dating are the same as the other heroines, so reading the shared rules first makes it easier. This page focuses on what is different about Zoe and the 3 subscribers.

Trap: cut the title story and it is over

Title story messages must be answered, and leaving one on read or picking Maybe later or Politely decline ends that run's story. If you do not upload the first video in week 2, Zoe, Kayla and Amanda never show up at all.

Raising affinity#

They come as comments and DMs from week 4. With the influencer title, subscriber chatter comes separately, so there are lots of messages.

  • Good reply: the first one, praising her for finding the bug. Affinity goes up, but it costs a little stamina and stress.
  • Bad reply: the last one, brushing her off coldly. -10 in the first 5 messages, and up to -12 after you start dating.
  • In the donation message, Skip reading donation messages adds side progress but is affinity -10, a loss.
  • From dating on, replies that spend a little money (street food, a celebration cake) are the right ones.
Good to know: the influencer title means a flood of messages

With the influencer title, subscribers have their own slot, so up to 6 more messages a week come in. Each reply costs stamina and stress, so keep plenty of coffee and rest ☕

Crush → confession → dating#

  1. Crush invite (affinity 80, when you are not dating anyone)There's a cafe nearby that sells bug-shaped cookies → Sure. I'll squash the cookie bugs (+5). I don't meet subscribers... makes her leave.
  2. The crush stageDuring the crush stage affinity rises slowly. Keep up the dates and it fills to affinity 100.
  3. Confession (100)Bug report #100… → That bug stays. Let's date. (+5, stress -5). Decline and she leaves, and new relationships are locked for that run.

Marriage conditions#

Date progress 60 + affinity 100 while dating brings the proposal: Bug report #365. Expected: living together forever. → Approved. Shipping the hotfix. (+10, stress -8). I'm not ready yet is -15 and it never comes again. Married at week 100 means the marriage ending.

How long until the proposal

The proposal comes once date progress reaches 60 (Brooke alone needs 20). The more often you schedule dates, the faster it fills.

Compared with Kayla ($815K) and Amanda ($615K), Zoe's dream home is $177K, so she is the easiest one for going after the living together + newlyweds + marriage endings all at once.

FAQ#

Can I meet Zoe without the title?
No. All of Zoe's messages only come in runs with the Dev influencer title equipped.
Does Zoe disappear if I change jobs?
No. She is not a coworker but a channel subscriber, so she keeps in touch whatever your company.
What if I only date her and finish the 100 weeks?
The 3 subscribers have no dating ending, so no Zoe ending comes. You have to go all the way to marriage.
What if I have crushes on Kayla and Amanda at the same time?
From 2 crushes on, the juggling can get caught every week (5% with 2, 8% with 3), and the confession comes first from whoever hits affinity 100 first. Once you date one, the other crushes quietly end.
