Heroine guides

All 13 heroines compared at a glance, who to pick, and the rules shared by every heroine. Your doorway to each heroine's guide

Last updated 2026-11-20

In short
  • 💌 There are 13 heroines in all: 6 everyday heroines (Emily, Rachel, Megan, Chloe, Brooke, Lauren), 4 title heroines (Hannah, Zoe, Kayla, Amanda) and 3 company heroines (Sophie, Claire, Angie).
  • 🔰 On your first run, go for Emily (she messages you first in week 2). For a cheap path all the way to the taste ending, Megan and Chloe; the biggest codex rewards come from Brooke (a Mythic crew member) and Angie and Hannah (Legendary Packs).
  • 🏠 Only 9 can live with you or have newlywed life, and Hannah, Sophie, Claire and Angie have no children. Check the table below before you choose.
  • 📌 The rules are mostly the same: warm replies to her weekly messages, −15 for leaving her on read, −30 for a week with 0 dates while dating, −30 for a week with 0 chores after marriage.

Rules shared by every heroine 📌#

Each heroine has her own lines and her own pace of affinity, but the skeleton is the same. Before a crush and during the crush stage affinity rises slowly; from dating on it rises exactly as the reply says. The weekly to-dos and the overflow love effects are in Rules shared by every heroine.

  1. Meeting → crush invite (affinity 80)Before a crush, affinity stops at 80. The crush invite message comes the week it hits 80, so always accept. Decline it or leave it on read and that heroine leaves.
  2. Crush → confession (affinity 100)Turn down the confession or leave it on read and she leaves, and new relationships are locked for that run.
  3. Dating → proposal (date progress 60 + affinity 100)Progress only builds through dates (Brooke alone needs just 20). Turning down the proposal is −15, and it never comes again.
  4. Marriage → week 100Marriage and dating endings are only recorded if you are still in that state at week 100. Breaking up or divorcing along the way voids them.
When new relationships get locked

Declining a crush invite only ends things with that heroine; you can still meet the others. But once you are in a crush, turning down a confession, breaking up while dating or divorcing means no new relationship can start in that run.

Every week, make sure toIf you don't
Reply to her messagesLeft on read −15 (clearing notifications counts as leaving on read too)
At least 1 date while dating−30 for a week with 0 + an I'm hurt message
At least 1 chore after marriage (and parenting if you have a child)−30 for each week with 0. At affinity 0 comes Let's get a divorce (30% chance to hold on)
Rest or dates on weekends instead of workGirlfriend, wife −2
Only 1 crushWith 2 or more crushes and no partner, there is a chance every week the juggling gets caught (5% with 2, 8% with 3, then +5% for each one more)
Trap: a company heroine is gone when you leave the company

Emily and Rachel are at the first company, Sophie at the foreign company, Claire at the startup and Angie at Morning Commit. Leave that company before a crush and contact stops (with Claire, leaving the startup ends it even past the crush stage). Megan, Chloe and Lauren are from university, so the company does not matter.

Several at once?

There are also juggling endings, where you keep crushes going with several heroines. If you get caught, everyone leaves and reputation is locked at 0, so the Guilty conscience gear is all but a must. See the ending codex's juggling endings and Gear for details.

FAQ#

Can I marry several heroines in one run?
No. Once you start dating someone, every other crush ends. The basic plan is one heroine per run.
Why don't the title heroines show up?
Hannah only appears if you equip the The patron title in that run, and Zoe, Kayla and Amanda only with Dev influencer. How to unlock titles is in Titles.
When do I meet Sophie, Claire and Angie?
They contact you in your first week at their company, after joining through a recruiter's offer. They do not come in a run where new relationships are locked because you already turned down a confession, broke up or divorced. How to change companies is in Promotions, career.
Do I have to live together after marriage?
No. Living together and newlywed life are optional. But chores are needed every week after marriage, whether you live together or not.
